Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Your privacy is our unwavering commitment—we employ enterprise-grade encryption and never share your data with unauthorized third parties. At Sentiment Analytics, we process massive scale data without compromising individual integrity.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We believe trust is earned through transparency; our practices are audited regularly to ensure your information remains secure and under your control. When you interact with our AI logic engine, we only collect telemetry strictly necessary for sentiment classification and pipeline execution.

2. Security Measures

All data at rest and in transit is protected using AES-256 encryption. We utilize isolated tensor processing environments to ensure that any analyzed text streams are immediately anonymized.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Aggregated metadata is only shared with verified partners when required for API operability, governed by stringent Data Processing Agreements.
