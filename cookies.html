info 1. What Are Cookies

summarize TL;DR

Cookies are tiny files saved to your computer that help websites remember things about you (like if you're logged in).

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you access the internet. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

settings 2. How We Use Cookies

summarize TL;DR

We use cookies to make Sentio run smoothly, remember your dark mode settings, and measure how fast our servers are responding. We do not use intrusive cross-site ad-tracking.

At Sentio, we use cookies primarily to ensure platform stability and remember your UI preferences. We do not use cookies for intrusive cross-site ad-tracking. Below are the specific ways we use these technologies on our real-time sentiment analysis dashboard:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for you to browse our dashboard and use its features. We rely on them for secure session management and to facilitate the secure fetching of API data. Without these cookies, the core functionality of Sentio cannot be provided.

Functional Cookies

These allow our platform to remember choices you make while browsing. For example, we use functional cookies to remember your visual preferences (such as maintaining our Dark Mode, glassmorphism aesthetic across sessions) and for caching your recent "Head-to-Head Battle Mode" inputs to provide a smoother, faster experience.

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our dashboard by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data strictly to monitor dashboard load times, track API latency, and identify UI bottlenecks so we can optimize our telemetry engine.

Third-Party Cookies

Because Sentio integrates with advanced external APIs, some of these embedded services may place their own cookies on your device to render correctly. Specifically, our Geo-Spatial maps rely on Mapbox/Leaflet, which may use cookies to load map tiles efficiently. We have no direct control over the information collected by these third-party cookies.

tune 3. Managing Preferences

summarize TL;DR

You can turn off cookies in your browser, but if you do, some important features of the Sentio dashboard might break.

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by adjusting the settings in your web browser. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, accept all cookies, or be notified before a cookie is set.

warning Important Note

If you choose to disable or block our Strictly Necessary cookies, please be aware that certain features of the Sentio dashboard—particularly the real-time data streaming and secure API connections—may break or function improperly.
